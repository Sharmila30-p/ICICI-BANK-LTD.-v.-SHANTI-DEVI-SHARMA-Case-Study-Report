# ICICI-BANK-LTD.-v.-SHANTI-DEVI-SHARMA-Case-Study-Report

This repository contains a case study report on ICICI Bank Ltd. v. Shanti Devi Sharma (2008). The report discusses the use of recovery agents by banks, the legal and ethical responsibilities of financial institutions, and the protection of borrowers’ rights.

The case emphasizes that banks must recover loans through lawful procedures and must not use force, threats, harassment, or abusive behaviour. It also highlights the importance of following Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines, properly supervising recovery agents, and treating customers with dignity and respect.

Key topics covered:

*Facts and background of the case.

*Legal issues considered by the court.

*Supreme Court judgment and legal principles.

*Fair loan recovery procedures.

*RBI guidelines and recovery-agent responsibilities.

*Borrower protection and responsible banking practices.

*Business relevance of the judgment.
